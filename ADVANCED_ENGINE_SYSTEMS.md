# Advanced Engine Systems

Implemented reusable systems:

1. Advanced AI — state-based detection, chase, attack, search, patrol.
2. Tilemaps — grid maps, solid cells, rendering.
3. Advanced Sprite Sheets — frame animation and reusable animator.
4. Dialogue — speaker, lines, progression, interaction.
5. Inventory — stackable item quantities and item checks.
6. Quest System — collect/defeat objectives and completion.
7. Boss System — health, phases, cooldowns, death.
8. Vehicle Physics — acceleration, braking, friction, steering, drift.

Additional validation games: Dungeon Quest, Haunted Manor, Castle Siege, Alien Invasion, Farm Defender, Treasure Hunter, Samurai Arena, Rally Rush, Delivery Driver, Dragon's Keep.

These systems are shared modules under `framework/advanced-systems.js` and are exercised through `framework/advanced-arcade-game.js`.
